# Code of Conduct

ProtoCarry welcomes participation regardless of background, identity or experience. Treat other participants with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints, personal boundaries and privacy.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep discussion relevant and make room for other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publication of private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer, [@0then0](https://github.com/0then0). If no private contact is available, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without naming affected people or posting sensitive details. Do not use a public thread to disclose private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports should be handled with discretion; information may need to be shared to investigate and respond. A participant may ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision and provide relevant context.

These expectations also apply when someone represents ProtoCarry in other spaces. They do not require agreement on technical decisions; disagreement must remain respectful.
